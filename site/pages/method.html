How this works

What this is

A free, neutral guide to substance use treatment and recovery in Southeast Wisconsin. It starts from the state's license records, then adds honest notes from people who have used these programs.

What it is not

  • It is not run by, endorsed by or affiliated with Alcoholics Anonymous, Narcotics Anonymous, any treatment program or any government agency.
  • It takes no referral fees, no paid placement and no money from facilities. A program cannot buy a better listing or a better review.
  • It collects no health information about you. There are no accounts and no intake forms.

How reviews are written

  • Every review is signed. The reviewer discloses any connection to the program, in plain words, at the top.
  • Claims such as "takes BadgerCare" say how they were confirmed and when. If we have not confirmed it, the page says so and tells you to call.
  • Notes from other people in recovery appear with a name, a first name or no name, depending on what each person chose. Nobody is identified without their consent.

If a program disagrees

Programs can ask us to correct a factual error. We will check it, fix what is wrong, and note the date. Opinions stay opinions, but they must be fair and based on first-hand experience.

Where the data comes from

License information comes from the Wisconsin Department of Health Services substance use provider directory. Maps come from OpenStreetMap contributors. Google review links open Google directly. We update the data weekly.
