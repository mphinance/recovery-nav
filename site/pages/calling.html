Calling a facility

The first call sets the tone. These questions work for anyone calling for themselves or for someone they love. You are allowed to ask all of them and to hang up if you do not get straight answers.

Ask every program

  1. Are you licensed by the state, and what is your license number?
  2. Do you take BadgerCare (Medicaid) for this level of care? Who checks my benefits, and when will I know what I owe?
  3. What will I pay out of pocket before I start, and what could I be billed later?
  4. Do you have a bed or an opening this week? If not, how long is the wait, and who can I call in the meantime?
  5. Is detox or withdrawal management on site, or do I need to go somewhere else first?
  6. Do you allow medications such as buprenorphine (Suboxone), naltrexone or methadone, and will you continue ones I already take?
  7. How long is the stay, and who decides when it ends?
  8. What happens if I relapse or leave early? Can I come back?
  9. Who owns the program, and do you pay anyone for referrals?
  10. What is the plan for after I leave: housing, counseling, follow-up?

Things to watch for

  • A call center that will not say which facility it works for.
  • Pressure to decide today, or "the bed will be gone."
  • Free flights or travel offers to a place far from home.
  • Refusing to say a price, or taking only private insurance when you have BadgerCare.
  • A form that asks for a Social Security number before anyone has told you what the program offers.

This list is general information, not medical advice. If you or someone you know is in danger right now, call 988 or 911.
